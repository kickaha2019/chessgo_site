## Clubs for Children

*Cambridge Junior Chess & Go Club* - this club! - We have meetings on Wednesdays at St Augustine's Church hall in the north of Cambridge.

The [Anglia Learning Chess Academy](https://sawstonvc.org/anglian-learning-chess-academy/){:target="_blank"}
 meets on Fridays during term time at Sawston Village College and 
 is aimed at Year 6 and over (not beginners). The meetings are from 
 6pm-8pm. For more information contact Paul Kemp (paulkemp64 at 
 gmail dot com)

The online club 
[Chess Steps](https://www.facebook.com/stepintochess/){:target="_blank"}
runs on Fridays at 5pm and Saturdays at 6:30pm. Each session is one hour. They have many children from Cambourne and Cambridgeshire attending. *Chess Steps* also offer online one to one chess coaching.

[Cherry Hinton Chess Club](https://ecf.justgo.com/weblets/CoachAndClubFinder/124c8f3b-1e94-44fc-8217-e70f15953111/Club/details/9AD951C3-12B4-4ACC-A202-E6497C05B5CD?page=1){:target="_blank"}
have chess sessions at Cherry Hinton Hall on Saturday afternoons. These are in three levels: Beginners 12-1pm, Improvers 1-2pm, Intermediate/Advanced: 3-4:15pm. For more information contact Daniel (chess4rookies at gmail dot com).

[The Cambridge University Go Society](https://www.facebook.com/cugosoc/){:target="_blank"}
has been running some teaching sessions for children on Sunday 
afternoons from 2:30pm to 4pm. These usually only run in the autumn 
and spring terms but there will probably be a few sessions after 
Easter. Contact paul361smith at gmail dot com for more information.

Many local schools have chess clubs and/or teams, including King's, 
St Faiths, St Matthews, Sancton Wood, The Perse, Cambourne Village 
College, Soham Village College, Newnham Croft, St Pauls, St Johns, 
Olive Academies and Milton. There are Go clubs at St Faiths, the 
Perse Upper, Milton and St Mary's. There's a Chess & Go Club at the 
Stephen Perse Foundation.

## Private Lessons
[Chess Steps](https://www.facebook.com/stepintochess/){:target="_blank"}
offer online one-to-one chess coaching.

Paul Smith (paul361smith at gmail dot com)
offers private Go lessons to individuals or groups, either in person 
or online. He can also do a one-off introductory session to a group 
of children of the rules of any of these games: Go, Shogi, Chinese 
Chess, Oware.
